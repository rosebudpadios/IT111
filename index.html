What Is Digital Character?

Digital character is the way we show our values, attitudes, and behavior when using technology and interacting with others online. It means doing what is right even when no one is watching.

Core Values of Digital Character

🤝

Respect

Use kind words, consider others' feelings, and avoid cyberbullying.

✅

Honesty

Share truthful information, give credit to creators, and avoid plagiarism.

🛡️

Responsibility

Protect personal data, think before posting, and use technology wisely.

Simple Ways to Practice

  • Think before you post or comment.
  • Check the facts before sharing information.
  • Ask permission before posting someone else's photo.
  • Use strong passwords and keep them private.
  • Speak up or seek help when you see harmful online behavior.

Your Digital Footprint Matters

Every click, post, and message is an opportunity to show good character.
